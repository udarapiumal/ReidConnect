# ReidConnect

**A Smart Campus Platform for the University of Colombo School of Computing (UCSC)**

ReidConnect is a full-stack smart campus platform developed as a university final year project under the close mentorship of UCSC's Director and Senior Assistant Registrar (SAR). It aims to transform campus life by unifying core university operations into a single digital ecosystem.

## Overview

Built to serve 900+ students, ReidConnect digitizes and streamlines everyday academic and administrative processes — from timetable generation to document approvals — while also enhancing student community engagement through a dedicated mobile app.

## Key Features

###  Automated Timetable Generation
Intelligent scheduling engine with clash detection across courses, lecturers, and venues, designed to handle the complex requirements of 900+ students.

###  Digital Venue Booking
Streamlined reservation system for lecture halls, labs, and event spaces, reducing manual coordination overhead.

###  PKI-Based Document Signing
Public Key Infrastructure-driven digital signatures that eliminate paper-based approval workflows, enabling secure and verifiable document approvals.

###  Club Management
Tools for managing clubs, memberships, and subscriptions across the university's student societies.

###  Lost and Found System
Centralized platform for reporting, tracking, and recovering lost items on campus.

###  Mobile App
- Community feeds for campus-wide engagement
- Event publishing and discovery
- Personalized academic and extracurricular tracking

###  Report Generation
Automated generation of reports to support administrative decision-making and oversight.
